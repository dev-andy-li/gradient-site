Questions, problems, and adding your district
Email dev.andy.li@icloud.com. In Gradient, Settings → Send feedback opens an email with your app and iOS versions filled in. Never send your password.

My district isn't in the list

Add it with the link you sign in at:

  1. In Safari, open the page where you sign in to Home Access Center. Your school's website usually links to it as "HAC" or "Home Access Center".
  2. Tap the address bar and copy the link. It usually has /HomeAccess in it, like hac.yourdistrict.org/HomeAccess.
  3. In Gradient, tap District on the sign-in page, then Add my district. Paste the link and tap Check link.

If it doesn't work, tap Email for help there, or email the address above with:

  • Your district's name, and its city and state
  • The link you sign in at, or your school's website
  • What happened, or the message Gradient showed

"This district signs in through ClassLink" (or Clever, Microsoft, Google)

Some districts sign in to Home Access Center through another site. Gradient can't do that sign-in yet. Email your district's name so it can be worked on.

My grades look wrong

Home Access Center is the official record. Pull down or tap refresh to load them again. If something still doesn't match, email which class and what you see in each place, without your password.

Gradient Supporter

To cancel, open your iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Gradient. To get Supporter back on a new phone, open the Supporter page in Gradient and tap Restore. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Removing your data

Settings → Students → Remove, or Sign out, deletes that login and its cached grades from your phone. Deleting the app removes everything. See the privacy policy.
