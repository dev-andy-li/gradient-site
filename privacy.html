Gradient

Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026
In short: Gradient, on iPhone and Android, doesn't collect your personal information. Your Home Access Center login and your grades stay on your phone. There's no Gradient account, no Gradient server, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

  • Your Home Access Center username and password. Saved in the iPhone Keychain, only on this device (not synced to iCloud or included in backups to other devices). On Android, they're sealed with a key in the Android Keystore and left out of backups. They're sent only to your district's Home Access Center, to sign you in.
  • Your grades and school information (classes, assignments, schedule, attendance, report cards) that Gradient loads from Home Access Center. Kept in a cache on your phone so the app opens quickly and works offline.
  • Your settings, such as your theme, app icon, App Lock choice, and the districts you've added. An App Lock PIN is kept in the Keychain (the Keystore on Android).

None of this is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Who Gradient talks to

  • Your district's Home Access Center. Gradient connects straight from your phone to your district's site, the same way Safari would, to sign in and load your grades. Your district's own privacy policies apply to that site.
  • The Gradient website (this site, hosted by GitHub Pages). Gradient downloads the list of districts from here now and then. It's a plain download: nothing about you is sent. Like any website, GitHub may log the download's IP address; see GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Apple, if you buy Gradient Supporter. The App Store handles the purchase. The developer receives no payment details and never learns who you are. Gradient checks whether you're a Supporter using Apple's signed records on your phone.

New-grade alerts are created on your phone by Gradient itself. They don't go through any server.

Crash reports

Gradient has no analytics or crash reporting of its own. On iPhone, if you've chosen to share analytics with app developers (Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple may pass the developer anonymous crash reports: things like the iPhone model, the iOS version, the time, and what the app was doing when it crashed. They don't include your login or your grades. The Android app sends no crash reports.

Cookies

Gradient doesn't use cookies of its own, and there are no tracking cookies. When you sign in, Home Access Center gives Gradient a sign-in cookie, the same way it gives one to a browser. Gradient keeps it on your phone to stay signed in and sends it only back to your district's site.

Security

Gradient talks to your district's Home Access Center the way your browser does, so your grades are as secure there as they are on your district's own site. On your phone, they're as secure as your phone is: see "Keeping your phone secure" in the terms.

Links to other sites

Gradient and this site link to other sites, such as your district's Home Access Center, Apple and GitHub. Only this site is run by the developer. Other sites have their own content and privacy policies, which the developer doesn't control and isn't responsible for.

Email

If you email for help or feedback, the developer sees your email address and whatever you write, and uses it only to answer you and to fix or add your district. It isn't shared or sold. Please never send your password. Ask any time and your emails will be deleted.

Children and students

Gradient is made for students and their families. Because Gradient doesn't collect personal information from anyone, it doesn't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Parents or guardians with questions can email the address below.

Deleting your information

Remove a student in Gradient (Settings → Students), or sign out, to delete that login and its cached grades from your phone. Deleting the app removes everything Gradient stored.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to know, access, correct or delete personal information a company holds about you. Gradient holds none, apart from any emails you choose to send, which you can ask to have deleted. Gradient doesn't sell or share personal information.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The new version will be posted here with a new date. A change that collects anything new would be announced in the app first.
