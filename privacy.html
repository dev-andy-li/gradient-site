Gradient

Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026
Summary: Gradient collects zero personal information, analytics, or telemetry. Your HAC login and grades are stored only on your device. There are no servers, tracking, or ads.

Data stored on your phone

  • Your Home Access Center username and password. Your login (optionally stored in the iOS Keychain or the Android Keystore) is kept on device for fast log-ins. They're sent only to your district's Home Access Center to sign you in.
  • Your grades and school information (classes, assignments, schedule, attendance, report cards) loaded from HAC are kept in a cache on your phone so the app opens quickly and works offline.
  • Your settings, such as your theme, app icon, App Lock choice, and the districts you've added are stored so they stay after closing the app.

None of this is sent to the developer or anywhere else.

Who Gradient communicates with

  • Your district's Home Access Center. Gradient connects straight from your phone to your district's site (the same way Safari does) to sign in and load your grades.
  • The Gradient website (this site). Gradient downloads the list of districts from here now and then. It's a simple download and nothing about you is sent. Like any website, GitHub may log the download's IP address; see GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Apple, if you buy the Gradient Supporter subscription. The App Store handles the purchase. I, the developer, receive no payment details and no personal information. Gradient checks whether you're a Supporter using Apple's signed records on your phone.

Crash reports

Gradient has no analytics or crash reporting on its own. On iPhone, if you've chosen to share analytics with app developers (Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple may pass the developer anonymous crash reports with things like the iPhone model, the iOS version, the time, and what the app was doing when it crashed. They don't include your login or your grades.

Cookies

Gradient does not use any cookies by itself, including tracking cookies. When you sign in, Home Access Center gives Gradient a sign-in cookie, the same way it gives one to a browser. Gradient keeps it on your phone to stay signed in and sends it only back to your district's site.

Security

Gradient communicates with your district's HAC exactly how your browser does, so your grades are as secure there as they are on your district's own site. On your phone, they're as secure as your phone is: see "Liability and keeping your info secure" in the terms.

Links to other sites

Gradient and this site link to other sites, such as your district's Home Access Center, Apple and GitHub. Only this site is run by me, the developer. Other sites have their own content and privacy policies, which I don't control and am not responsible for.

Email

If you email for help or feedback, I will see your email address and whatever you write, and I'll only use it to help you with your issues. Your information is never shared or sold. Please never send your password or any personal information. Ask any time and your emails will be deleted.

Children and students

Gradient is made for students. Because Gradient does not collect personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Parents or guardians with questions can email the address below.

Deleting your information

Remove a student in Gradient (Settings → Students) or sign out to delete that login and its cached grades from your phone. Deleting the app removes everything stored in the app.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to know, access, correct or delete personal information a company holds about you. Gradient holds none, apart from any emails you choose to send, which you can ask to have deleted. Gradient does not sell or share personal information.

Changes
