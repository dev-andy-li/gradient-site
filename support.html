Support/FAQ

Questions, problems, and adding your district
For questions, support, or feedback, email dev.andy.li@icloud.com. Gradient > Settings > Send feedback also works.

My district isn't in the list

To add your district:

  1. Open the page where you sign in to Home Access Center in a web browser. It should be titled or labeled as "HAC" or "Home Access Center".
  2. Tap the address bar and copy the entire link. It should be something like hac.yourdistrict.org/HomeAccess.
  3. In Gradient, tap District on the sign-in page, then Add my district. Paste the link and tap Check link.

If it doesn't work, tap Email for help there, or email the address above with:

  • Your district's name, and its city and state
  • The link you sign in at, or your school's website
  • What happened, or the message Gradient showed

I will usually respond within 48 hours.

"This district signs in through ClassLink" (or Clever, Microsoft, Google)

Some districts sign in to Home Access Center through another site. If so, this app can't work for your district yet. Email me your district's name and SSO (the site it uses for sign-ins, like Clever or Microsoft) and I'll work on it as soon as I can.

My grades look wrong

Home Access Center is the official record, and Gradient may display incorrect info due to rounding differences or network issues. Tap refresh to load them again. If something still doesn't match, email me which class and what the issue is.

Gradient Supporter

To cancel the subscription, open your iPhone's Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Gradient. To get Supporter back on a new phone, open the Supporter page in Gradient and tap Restore. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Removing your data

Go to Settings > Students > Remove/Sign out. This permanently deletes that login and its cached grades from your phone. Deleting the app removes all information. See the privacy policy for more info.
